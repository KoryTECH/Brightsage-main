01

Introduction

This page describes how Brightsage Ezzential ("Brightsage", "we", "our") handles personal information collected through our website and in the course of our professional engagements. It is maintained by Brightsage and applies to visitors, prospective clients and clients.

02

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, company, role, email address, telephone number and the content of enquiries you submit. In the course of client engagements we may also handle financial, tax and operational information required to deliver our services.

03

How we use information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, deliver contracted services, meet our professional and regulatory obligations, and communicate updates or executive briefings where you have opted in. We do not sell personal information.

04

Confidentiality

All client information is treated as strictly confidential and privileged. Access is limited to professionals directly involved in the engagement and bound by professional codes of conduct.

05

Data retention

We retain personal and engagement information for the period required by professional standards, contractual obligations and applicable Nigerian law, after which it is securely destroyed or anonymised.

06

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us. Requests can be directed to brightsageezzential@gmail.com.

07

Contact

For any questions regarding this policy, please contact our office at brightsageezzential@gmail.com or via the details on our Contact page.

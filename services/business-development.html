Business Development

Strategic growth architecture for ambitious enterprises.

Executive overview

We work alongside founders, boards and executive teams to design, evaluate and execute growth strategy grounded in disciplined financial modelling and multinational operating experience.

Business problems solved

Where clients typically call us in.

  • Ambitious growth plans lacking financial substantiation
  • Unclear market positioning and revenue architecture
  • Weak commercial governance and pipeline discipline
  • Underperforming subsidiaries or product lines
Benefits

What clients gain.

  • Board-ready growth strategy grounded in numbers
  • Clear commercial governance and KPI architecture
  • Executive alignment on capital allocation
  • Predictable, measurable revenue expansion
Our process

How the engagement runs.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic

    Full commercial, financial and operational assessment of the current business.

  2. 02

    Strategy design

    Executive workshops to define market position, target segments and growth thesis.

  3. 03

    Financial modelling

    Bottom-up modelling to validate every assumption and expose fragile economics.

  4. 04

    Execution roadmap

    Sequenced 90 / 180 / 365-day plan with owners, milestones and reporting cadence.

Deliverables

What you receive.

  • 01Executive growth strategy document
  • 02Integrated three-statement financial model
  • 03Commercial KPI dashboard
  • 04Board and investor presentation pack

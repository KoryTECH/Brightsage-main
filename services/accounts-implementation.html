Accounts Implementation

Accounting systems, controls and reporting that hold up under scrutiny.

From chart-of-accounts design to full ERP deployment, we install the accounting infrastructure that turns your finance function into a source of clarity, control and confidence.

Common problems

  • Fragmented spreadsheets and inconsistent reporting
  • No audit trail, weak internal control environment
  • Chart of accounts unfit for management reporting
  • Manual month-end close taking weeks, not days

What changes

  • Single, trusted source of financial truth
  • Automated month-end close in days
  • Audit-ready books and reconciliations
  • Real-time visibility for executive decision-making

How we work

  1. 01

    As-is review

    Structured review of existing systems, processes and control weaknesses.

  2. 02

    Design

    New chart of accounts, control matrix and reporting architecture.

  3. 03

    Implementation

    System configuration, data migration and staff training.

  4. 04

    Post go-live

    First three closes shadowed by our senior team until steady state.

Deliverables

  • Redesigned chart of accounts and policy manual
  • Configured accounting / ERP environment
  • Documented internal control framework
  • Trained finance team and operations manual

  • Engineering
  • Oil & Gas
  • Growing Businesses

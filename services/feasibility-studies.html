Services/Feasibility Studies

Feasibility Studies & Project Management

Feasibility studies and project management for capital decisions.

For capital projects, new products, market entries and investment decisions, we deliver rigorous feasibility studies and lead disciplined project execution against them.

Common problems

  • Capital committed to projects with weak underlying economics
  • Optimistic assumptions untested against downside
  • Projects that slip on time, budget and quality
  • No credible study to support debt or equity raise

What changes

  • Lender-ready feasibility studies
  • Honest downside and sensitivity analysis
  • Structured execution with disciplined governance
  • Confidence to commit, or to decline, capital

How we work

  1. 01

    Scoping

    Define the decision the study must inform and the standards it must meet.

  2. 02

    Study

    Market, technical, financial, legal and risk analysis.

  3. 03

    Report

    Executive-quality feasibility report and financial model.

  4. 04

    Execute

    PMO leadership through implementation to steady-state operations.

Deliverables

  • Full feasibility study report
  • Integrated financial model with sensitivities
  • Project execution plan and governance framework
  • Project management office (PMO) leadership
